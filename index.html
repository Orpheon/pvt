How to read this

The test measures how quickly you notice and tap a counter that appears at random intervals. Sleep loss slows the average, and above all makes the slow tail slower: more lapses (taps slower than 355 ms) and more false starts. Response speed is 1 divided by reaction time in seconds, so higher is better and it is less distorted by the occasional very slow tap than mean reaction time is.

Compare yourself against yourself. Same phone, same posture, same time relative to waking, before caffeine. A 3-minute test underestimates real-world impairment because the vigilance decrement grows with time on task, so treat the line as a trend, not a fitness check. Absolute numbers include your phone's touch latency and will not match published lab values.

Protocol after Basner, Mollicone & Dinges (2011), PVT-B: 3 min, 1–4 s inter-stimulus interval, lapse ≥ 355 ms, false start = tap before the stimulus or within 100 ms of it.
